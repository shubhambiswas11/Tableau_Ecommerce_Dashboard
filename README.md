## 🎯 Project Objectives & Business Value
This dashboard answers key business questions to help stakeholders track performance and spot trends:
* **Revenue Operations:** What are the Year-over-Year (YoY) sales, profit, and order trends?
* **Product Analytics:** Which product categories grew or declined vs. last year?
* **Geographical Insights:** Which regions drive the most sales, and how is market share distributed?
* **Customer Behavior:** How does order volume by region shift month-to-month, and when do acquisition peaks occur?

* **YTD Order Quantity:** 157,978 orders (+15.02% vs previous year)
* **Market Share by Region:** % contribution to sales by Europe, LATAM, Pacific Asia, Africa, USCA
* **Category-wise YTD vs Previous YTD Sales:** growth/decline % for top 10 categories

---

## 💡 Key Insights
* **Profit growing slower than sales:** Sales grew 5.04% YoY, but profit actually *declined* 0.46% — suggests rising costs or discounting is eating into margins even as volume grows.
* **Sales concentration:** LATAM (45.86%) and Europe (43.37%) together account for ~89% of total sales — the business is highly dependent on just two regions.
* **Seasonal acquisition pattern:** Customer acquisition (order volume) across most regions peaks around Nov–Jan, consistent with festive-season shopping behavior.
* **Category-level divergence:** Some top-selling categories (by revenue) are still declining YoY, while smaller categories are growing — worth flagging for inventory/marketing prioritization.
